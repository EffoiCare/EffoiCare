
# EffoiCare 🇪🇹

> **Empowering Ethiopian mothers and caregivers of children with autism and developmental delays through an offline-first, culturally resonant digital support platform.**
## Project Overview
In Addis Ababa and urban centers across Ethiopia, families raising children with autism and developmental delays face an acute shortage of accessible clinical and therapeutic support infrastructure. Mothers bear the immense burden of unassisted, 24/7 caregiving, leading to chronic stress, social isolation, and economic inactivity. 

**EffoiCare** is an offline-first digital solution designed to bridge the vast divide between formal healthcare facilities and daily household care. By equipping caregivers with actionable, evidence-based daily strategies, our platform reduces developmental regression in children, lowers caregiver burnout, and paves the way for mothers to reclaim their time and return to economic activity.
## Key Features
* **Offline-First and free video Architecture:** Tailored for regions with intermittent connectivity. Core educational modules and tracking tools are stored locally on the device, ensuring uninterrupted daily use without requiring costly data plans.
* **Localized Content & Guidance:** Evidence-based frameworks translated into culturally resonant formats covering child education, specialized nutrition, hyperactivity control, and milestone tracking.
* **Caregiver Stress Reduction:** Intuitive digital tools designed to provide structured home guidance, alleviating isolation and anxiety for mothers.
* **Scalable Framework:** Optimized for lower-end Android/mobile devices with high penetration rates in Ethiopian households.
## Technical Stack & Architecture
* **Platform:** Cross-platform Mobile Application (optimized for low-end devices).
* **Data Persistence:** Local storage/offline caching layer ensuring complete functionality without active internet connectivity.
* **Design Philosophy:** Lightweight, accessible UI/UX tailored for high usability among caregivers with varying digital literacy levels.

## Key Partners & Advisors
